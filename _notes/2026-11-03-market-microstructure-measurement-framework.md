This is a template placeholder for a future research note. Replace the prompts below with the actual research design, evidence, and conclusions.

## Research question

State the market behavior or trading mechanism being investigated, why it matters, and the hypothesis to be tested.

## Data and sample

Describe the data source, instruments, venue, sampling frequency, study period, filters, and known coverage limitations.

## Measurement design

Define the primary variables and estimators. Explain choices such as quote alignment, trade classification, spread measurement, and aggregation windows.

## Validation checks

List data-quality tests, alternative definitions, subsample comparisons, and sensitivity analyses that should be run before interpreting the results.

## Findings

Summarize the empirical results here. Separate robust evidence from tentative patterns and note any conflicting measurements.

## Takeaways

Record the practical implications, limitations, and the next experiment that would most reduce uncertainty.
