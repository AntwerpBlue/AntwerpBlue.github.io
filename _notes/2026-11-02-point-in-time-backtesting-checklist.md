This is a template placeholder for a future research note. Use it to document a backtest before presenting performance statistics.

## Research question

Define the signal, investment universe, rebalance decision, and economic intuition in one concise statement.

## Point-in-time inputs

Document when every input became observable. Include reporting lags, universe membership, delistings, corporate actions, and revisions to historical data.

## Portfolio construction

Specify ranking, weighting, rebalance frequency, holding period, neutralization, exposure limits, and the treatment of missing observations.

## Costs and constraints

Describe commissions, spreads, market impact, borrow availability, turnover limits, capacity assumptions, and execution timing.

## Diagnostics

Check coverage, turnover, concentration, factor exposures, regime sensitivity, and whether results depend on a small number of securities or dates.

## Results

Report the main results, benchmark comparison, uncertainty estimates, and robustness tests. Add figures or tables only after the methodology is fixed.
